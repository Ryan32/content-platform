Principles

  1. 1Lead with lookups: one search box that jumps straight to a known document and sends questions to AXL.
  2. 2Use advisors’ own words. Keep the names that work (Coaching & Training, Events) and fix the two catch-all menus.
  3. 3Give carriers their own pages. Everything about Allianz in one place, grouped by job.
  4. 4Line of business, licensing, client-ready and format are filters, not menus.
  5. 5Apps (ChatAE, AE Portal, Leaderboards, Retirement Analyzer) go in an Apps launcher, separate from content.
  6. 6Home and quick links adapt to the person’s role from their AE Portal profile.

How we confirm it

  • Tree test: give 10 advisors and 10 staff the top 25 tasks and see where they click first. Target: 80% on the first try.
  • Confirm roles: check whether the portal’s Auth0 roles can tell advisors from staff. If not, ask once on first visit.
  • After launch: track what people search for and get no results, plus what AXL couldn’t answer, then adjust labels each month.
  • Fix the data: view counts are lifetime totals, so older items look stronger. Use the new library’s analytics from day one.
