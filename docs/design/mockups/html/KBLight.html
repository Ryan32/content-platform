Resolve a NIGO on an annuity case

Find out why a case is not in good order, upload what the carrier needs, and keep the case moving without calling the operations team.

How-to articleAnnuity5 min read·Updated Oct 2026 by Annuity Operations
NIGO means “not in good order”: the carrier needs something before it can issue the contract. Held at AE means our team is fixing it before it goes to the carrier.

Find cases that need you

Open Cases in AE Portal and filter by Needs action. Any case with an open requirement shows the cyan step in its tracker.

Resolve a requirementCopy link to this section

  1. 1
    Open the requirementSelect the case, then the requirement under Activity. The carrier’s note explains exactly what’s missing.
  2. 2
    Get the corrected documentMost NIGOs are a missing signature or date. Use the carrier’s current form: it’s on the carrier page in the Library.
  3. 3
    Upload and submitChoose Upload File, add the signed PDF and submit. Press Ctrl + V to paste a scanned image directly.

If you’re stuck

If the requirement doesn’t make sense, or it has been open for more than 5 business days, message Annuity Operations from the case. Include the case number so they can find it fast.
